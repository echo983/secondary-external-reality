---- Inv: SEVEN INVARIANTS ---------------------------------------

Inv:
I1{HardConsistency:      H_Ct ≠ ∅ ; hard commitments never mutually conflict}
I2{ExplanatoryAdmissibility:
     conflicting Evidence PERMITTED;
     ∃X: X ⊨ C_t ∧ ΔD<0 ∧ Complexity(X)<B ∧ TestableConsequences(X)>0}
I3{QueryIndependence:
     Query determines WHERE to reveal;
     never WHAT the unexposed past turns out to be}
I4{ProjectiveConsistency: A⊆B ⇒ ΠS(B)|A = ΠS(A)}
I5{CommitmentMonotonicity:
     μ / struct / exp roots and their closure never silently relaxed;
     forward change only via new Event on State; never deletion of past}
I6{CounterfactualStability:
     once operationally addressable, a projection holds a stable,
     query-path-independent counterfactual value — even while unexposed}
I7{MinimalCommitment:
     any resolution commits only MSRC — the minimal sufficient closure
     required for the current operation to be lawfully constituted}

Inv.note:
  I1–I6 ⇒ world does not drift.
  I7    ⇒ world does not freeze solid in unobserved regions
           as a side effect of not drifting.

LAYER N · PRECEDENT & RATIONALE
================================================================

NON-NORMATIVE. LOAD-BEARING.

Each entry: [idxref] · CLAIM · WHY. The WHY is the payload. A rule
without its counterexample gets re-proposed within months, because the
rejected version usually looks reasonable in isolation.


---- N.1 · REPEALED FORMULATIONS ---------------------------------

Ordered roughly by how costly re-proposing them would be.

[I1,I2 / P6] "player finds a contradiction ⇒ H_C = ∅"
  WHY REPEALED: conflates three different things — evidence conflicting
  with evidence, the current explanation failing, and committed hard facts
  being jointly unsatisfiable. Two witnesses contradicting each other does
  not mean no lawful history exists. Only the third is I1 failure, and only
  the third is unfixable by explanation.

[X1,X2] "find an upper-level explanation that makes all old evidence
         mutually consistent again"
  WHY REPEALED: evidence may conflict permanently. Witness A: red coat.
  Witness B: blue coat. The good model is "coat was red; B observed under
  blue lighting." Both pieces of evidence still disagree — what got
  explained is how they came to disagree. Requiring evidence to agree
  amounts to rewriting evidence, contradicting Evidence-immutable.

[X1] naming: "Truth Refactoring"
  WHY REPEALED: it does not refactor truth. It replaces the explanation
  model. The name invited exactly the error above. → Explanation Refactoring.

[Q2] "ER > 0 as an admission gate for Query"
  WHY REPEALED: ER = 0 is very often the correct answer. "Can I tell who
  gave her this necklace just by looking at it?" — no current epistemic
  path, and "you can't tell from the outside" is a perfectly good Boundary.
  Gating on ER classifies correct Boundaries as query failures.

[C5,n15] "fix before the projection could first enter external observation"
  WHY REPEALED: in a free-text world the player can ask about her twelfth
  birthday breakfast in the first second. Under theoretical askability,
  everything is addressable from world creation, and laziness dies
  entirely. → Operational Addressability: actual execution, actual
  canonical address.

[C5] "promote Free → fixed when in-edge count ≥ 2"
  WHY REPEALED: a single path queried twice on two different days demands
  the same stability. Multi-path reachability raises exposure risk; it is
  not the logical precondition of counterfactual fixation.

[C1,C3] Latent-fixed treated as OUTSIDE C_t
  WHY REPEALED: then I1 and I6 bite each other. Concretely:
    t1  κ17.met_year resolved to 2003 (unexposed)
    t2  another path commits "she was abroad 2001–2005"
    t3  player asks about the necklace
  I6 demands 2003; I1 demands re-collapse. Resolved by putting μ-root
  inside C_t: the t2 commitment is REJECTED AT t2, not discovered at t3.

[n12,n13] three-state gradient Free / Latent-fixed / Committed
  WHY REPEALED: it fused two orthogonal axes. "Has a value" and "who
  depends on it" vary independently. counterfactually-fixed + unexposed is
  a perfectly ordinary state and the old gradient had no room for it.

[n44] "Δ(unexplained evidence) < 0" as the refactoring test
  WHY REPEALED: counting is gameable. Seven small anomalies, then one new
  explanation — "there is a secret organization that can alter memories" —
  resolves all seven and introduces one. 7 → 1 looks excellent. The world
  just got much worse. Weight, don't count.

[n55] 0–4 ordered Commitment Class (Null < Query < Mark < Speech < Commit)
  WHY REPEALED: no general cost ordering exists. A Query can generate half
  a life. "Hello" is an Utterance and nearly free. A PhysicalAct can burn
  down a building. Semantic kind and price are independent dimensions.

[S4] "Commit is irrevocable" (as a statement about state)
  WHY REPEALED: E1044 breakup is irrevocable; Relationship(Peter, HMM)
  returning to romantic after E2018 reconciliation is normal. The event
  is permanent, the state it produced is not.

[M1,M2] memory field drifts directly to a wrong value
  WHY REPEALED: silent overwrite destroys the history of the memory
  itself. R91 recalled restaurant_23 with low confidence — that
  recollection HAPPENED, and a later recollection may differ. Both are
  events. Overwriting loses the trace and makes "she misremembers" a
  template rather than a computed consequence of encoding quality, age
  and interference.

[E1,E4] "any event perceived by any Agent creates an Event"
  WHY REPEALED: Event Ledger degenerates into a sensory log. Our original
  reason for the rule — "who saw it is the origin of all later epistemic
  propagation, miss it and you can't recover" — is satisfied instead by
  EpistemicAcquisition into the epistemic graph, with no world event.

[M5,M6] "compression is not deletion, it is returning degrees of freedom"
  WHY REPEALED: too broad; collides with I6. Must split into
  RepresentationalCompression (storage down, possibility set unchanged,
  re-query must recover the identical counterfactual result) and
  SemanticRelease (genuinely widening a domain — permitted only on Free
  Slack outside the closure and not counterfactually fixed).

[M8] "compression and promotion are the same operator in two directions"
  WHY REPEALED: Peter studied in Madrid in 1998 — settled for thirty
  years. He is elected president; Historical Load explodes; not one new
  constraint was added and the possibility set is untouched. Conversely,
  "what colour are her socks today" collapses a value while historical
  importance stays near zero. Two axes.

[n52 / Q4] "Redirect ≈ 0, Boundary ≈ 0"
  WHY REPEALED: "go ask her mother" is cheap only if the mother exists.
  "There's an old neighbour, gone for decades, who knows the truth"
  invents a person, a relationship and a history. Redirect is the most
  insidious cost leak precisely because it appears to generate nothing.
  Likewise "she never knew who her father was" is a heavy commitment.

[Q4] Refusal treated as uniformly free
  WHY REPEALED: "she has no brother" is free if C_t already entails it
  (EntailedRefusal), but costs a new μ-root if sibling_count was Free and
  is being fixed to 0 right now (ResolvedRefusal). Absence can be a new fact.

[S2] a single universal felicity rule
  WHY REPEALED: promise, breakup, refuse, forgive and confess have
  genuinely different standing and felicity conditions. One rule for all
  illocutions is wrong at the first case examined.

[n32] "inference is the only channel that cheaply produces correct-but-wrong
       beliefs"
  WHY REPEALED: mishearing, misidentification, unreliable testimony and
  faulty recall produce structurally sound false beliefs just as well.
  The important property is not the channel but that false beliefs are
  allowed to have VALID PROVENANCE.

[n80] "make the observable a finite set"
  WHY REPEALED: fine for fixed cameras, wrong as a general principle —
  a text world has unbounded potential queries. What is actually
  controlled is bandwidth: how many projections enter the evidence set
  per turn. Fixed camera limits it spatially; text limits it semantically.

[n81] "what is not described does not even count as undetermined"
  WHY REPEALED: "she is wearing a red dress" already entails a garment
  exists, is on her, is red where visible, and is dress-like. Omission
  leaves things constraint-defined but unresolved, not absent.

[V1] "the only zero-gap acceptance function"
  WHY REPEALED: overclaimed. Closest currently held, not unique and not
  provably zero-gap.

[V4] "Explanation Budget's three conditions are automatically checkable"
  WHY REPEALED: only once all three are confined to a managed formal
  language. Otherwise the verification semantic gap is still there.

[n43] "the CF ⚠ has been fully discharged by the constraint-store view"
  WHY REPEALED: over-optimistic. Pre-generation you can only bound.
  Answering "her mother" (already exists) versus "her university
  boyfriend" (needs person, relationship, interval, gift event, ownership
  history, memories) differ by orders of magnitude. Hence two-stage triage.

[T3] "resolve only the fields to be output"
  WHY REPEALED: produces dangling facts. Answering "Peter gave it to her"
  cannot commit only necklace.giver_name. It must also constitute the
  entity identity, the gift event, the giver edge and the item provenance
  relation — otherwise the Latent Entity Pool, projective consistency and
  referential integrity all break. → MSRC.

[APX-B Flow] "Flow needs two-sided hard constraints: sufficient AND not abundant"
  WHY REPEALED: a skilled player hoarding resources through good play must
  not render the world illegal. Survival lower bound is a hard constraint;
  scarcity pressure is a quality objective. Note this is an instance of P8:
  the lower bound is decidable, "too abundant" has no objective threshold.

[misc, earlier rounds]
  "dynamically generating a credible RE2 is impossible"
    → the ORIGINAL architecture was insufficient; with global design
      constraints it is feasible, though possibly not worth the complexity.
  "the trigger side决定一切"
    → too strong; duty also depends on assigned certainty, player
      investment, repeated confirmation, and whether a causal account exists.
  "consistency need only cover the trajectory paid for"
    → "only" too strong; players also judge by ANTICIPATED verifiability.
      A building outside the window that changes colour daily breaks trust
      even if never entered.
  "claims don't hit the ledger"
    → wrong. The utterance event is itself a hard fact. What stays free is
      the distant fact it refers to.
  "space should not exist in coordinate form"
    → rhetorical overreach. Three layers: coordinates below, capability
      abstraction in the middle, capability requests above.
  "what can be infinite is exactly what need not be remembered"
    → wrong. F(seed, coord) + sparse delta is a counterexample. Infinity is
      cheap; INCOMPRESSIBLE PERSISTENT CHANGE over an infinite region is not.
  "lazy determination is the only implementation of on-demand resolution"
    → too strong. deterministic_program(seed, query) is another route.
      Signed as: the most natural implementation in practice.
  "make the observer concede"
    → conceding is a psychological state, separable from verification.
      The standard is that the observer CAN VERIFY the boundary.
  "a convergent flaw is a reveal"
    → insufficient. Convergence can also be one repeated error. The test is
      whether convergence yields a CHECKABLE COMMON CAUSE.
  "Relationship State is the only strongly-consistent component"
    → wrong. Anything in the commitment closure demands consistency. The
      ring the player threw in the river last night cannot reappear in a
      drawer unexplained; that is the same violation as forgetting a breakup.
  "seeded commitment" as a standalone solution
    → strictly dominated by μ_S. Retained as the strongest of three
      seeded layers.
  "the exposed projection may not be relaxed" as the compression floor
    → insufficient. The floor is the whole Commitment Closure. A hidden
      wall the player never saw is already load-bearing if the system used
      it to establish that sound doesn't carry.

  t3: player asks about the necklace.

[Q7] THE ENGRAVING
  "Who gave her the necklace?" Bad answer: her ex-boyfriend — one field on
  paper, but the player leaves believing a whole person exists. Good
  answer: the inside of the ring is engraved "M. 2019" — a token, not a
  person. Tokens can stay unexplained indefinitely; people cannot.

[T3] DANGLING GIVER

[M1] R91

[E5] THE WAITER
  Did the waiter hear the argument? Never operationally addressable, so it
  stayed Free. Two hours later the player asks. ΠS resolves it from

  projection had simply never been evaluated.

[Q2] THE NECKLACE, LOOKED AT
  "Can I tell who gave it to her just by looking?" ER = 0 on the direct

[P3, strong-consistency] THE RING IN THE RIVER
  Player throws the ring in the river. It reappears in a drawer with no

[APX-B] THE HOARDING PLAYER
  Excellent play accumulates a large resource surplus. The world must not
  become illegal because of it.


---- N.3 · PROJECT DESIGN CONSTRAINTS ----------------------------

NOT theorems. Not derivable from P1–P12 / Inv. Project-level value
choices, recorded so implementers do not mistake them for invariants.

D1 · No preset morality INSIDE the world.
  The player may do wrong, be refused, lose, and regret. A character who
  cannot hate you cannot really accept you either.

D2 · Boundaries AT THE MACHINE LAYER.
  - leaving must be mechanically cheap
  - the fiction/reality line must not be blurred
  - no tuning against an individual's vulnerabilities to raise engagement

NOTE ON PROVENANCE: earlier drafts wrote that D1/D2 are "derived from
feasibility". They are not derivations. They are COGNATE with P1 and P3 —
a character who cannot refuse you and a world whose boundaries cannot be
collided with fail in the same way, and the failure mode of D2 is the same
as the detective-player-after-the-reveal case: once trust flips to
adversarial, it does not flip back. Cognate, not entailed.


---- N.4 · RESIDUAL DISAGREEMENT ---------------------------------

Can improvisation by AI outrun a human's limited attention?
  Breadth: yes, all parties agree.
  Depth: no, all parties agree.
  REASON: one improvisation need only fool one judgement. After a hundred
  interactions the model faces the constraint set jointly formed by all
  hundred. TIME TURNS COMMITMENTS INTO A TEST SET.
  Does not affect engineering conclusions; both positions imply the same
  resource allocation.


---- N.5 · WHAT THIS SYSTEM ACTUALLY AUTOMATES --------------------

Not the world. Given a world, it automates organizing an experience in it.

  offline / authored : place graph, asset primitives, behaviour DSL,
                       illocution registry, seeded commitments
  online / generated : motif selection & embedding (RE2 line), semantic
                       reduction, causal history, encounters, camera,
                       dialogue, on-demand historical resolution

This is not a retreat. Drawing a plausible police station is weeks of art.
Tuning it into a level with interlocking locks, an exact resource curve
and rewarding revisits is months of the least outsourceable design work
there is.


---- N.6 · CLOSING FORMULATION -----------------------------------

The world is a lazy possible world with a stable counterfactual structure,
developed only where required.

  Laziness applies to WHEN a fact is evaluated.
  Minimality applies to HOW MUCH is evaluated.
  Stability applies to the fact ONCE evaluated.

Four boundaries fixed:
  conflicting evidence is not a contradictory world
  forgetting cannot rewrite the past
  being perceived does not require entering world history
  the unexposed past may remain uninstantiated, but once operationally
    addressable it may not be re-decided to suit the player's present

Operating discipline, compressed:

  world holds large Free Slack
        ↓
  a projection becomes operationally addressable in actual execution
        ↓
  ΠS resolves the minimal sufficient closure
        ↓
  check, then atomically commit
        ↓
  only then expose
        ↓
  the new fact permanently joins the world's counterfactual structure
